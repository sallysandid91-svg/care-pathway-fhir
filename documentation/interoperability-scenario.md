# Interoperability Scenario

## Context

A hospital information system needs to exchange diabetes patient information with a regional health platform.

## Standards

- HL7 FHIR
- SNOMED CT
- LOINC

## Exchanged Resources

- Patient
- Encounter
- Condition
- ServiceRequest
- Observation

## Benefits

- Standardized data exchange
- Improved semantic interoperability
- Better data reuse for clinical care and research
